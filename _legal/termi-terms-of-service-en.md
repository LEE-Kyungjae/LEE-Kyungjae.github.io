## 1. Service scope

The Service lets users run an agent on a managed device and pair its Tailscale URL or other connection information with a web or mobile client. Only operating systems and architectures listed on the current download page are supported packages; other environments may require a separate build.

## 2. Authorization and security

Users may connect only to systems they own or are authorized to manage. Connection URLs, tokens, keys and accounts must be protected and rotated after suspected exposure. Sessions must be closed and stored connection data removed from shared devices. Applicable law, organizational security rules and target-system terms must be followed.

## 3. Remote commands and data

Terminal commands can modify or delete files, install software or transmit data. Users must verify commands and targets, back up important data, use least-privilege accounts in production and avoid exposing secrets. The Operator does not review or approve the suitability of each command entered by a user.
