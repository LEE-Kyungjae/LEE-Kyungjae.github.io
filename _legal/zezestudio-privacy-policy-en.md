This is the privacy policy for Ppituruppatura (the “Service”).

## 1. Information we collect

- Account information required for identification and service delivery
- Information required to process payments and deliver digital goods
- Usage history, access logs and error logs
- Contact information provided for support inquiries

## 2. How we use information

- Provide and operate the game service
- Deliver digital goods, confirm payments and process refunds
- Prevent abuse and respond to security incidents
- Respond to inquiries and deliver notices

## 3. Retention and deletion

- Information required by law is retained for the applicable statutory period.
- Information is deleted without delay when the retention period ends or its purpose is fulfilled.

## 4. Third-party sharing and processing

- Personal information is not shared with third parties without consent unless required by law.
- Applicable laws are followed when processing is delegated for payments, infrastructure or other operational needs.

## 5. Your rights

- You may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing for your personal information.
