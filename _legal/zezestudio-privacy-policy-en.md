ZaeZae Studio, represented by Kyungjae Lee (the “Operator”), operates Ppituruppatura (the “Service”) and processes personal information in accordance with Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws.

## 1. Information processed

| Context | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Account registration | Email address, encrypted password and username or nickname |
| Optional profile | Profile image and biography |
| Kakao login | Kakao user ID and the email, nickname or profile image the user agrees to share |
| Service activity | Gameplay, scores, rankings, play time, friend relationships and chat history |
| Automatically generated | IP address, cookies, access and error logs, device, browser and operating-system information |
| Purchases and refunds | Order number, purchased item, payment and refund history and payment status. Payment providers process card credentials; the Operator does not store full card details. |
| Support | Email address, inquiry, response history and user-provided attachments |

The Service does not intentionally collect personal information from children under 14 without the consent or other procedure required from a legal guardian. Information identified as having been collected without the required procedure will be deleted or handled through the legally required consent process.

## 2. Purposes

- Identify members, manage accounts and prevent abuse
- Provide gameplay, saved records, friends, chat and rankings
- Deliver digital content and process payments, withdrawal requests and refunds
- Diagnose failures, protect the Service and improve quality
- Handle inquiries, disputes and important notices
- Send marketing or event information only with separate optional consent

Refusing optional marketing consent does not prevent use of the core Service. Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

## 3. Retention

Personal information is deleted without undue delay when its purpose is fulfilled or the account is closed, except where retention is required by law or reasonably necessary for a dispute.

| Record | Period | Basis or purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Contracts and withdrawal requests | 5 years | Korean e-commerce law |
| Payments and supply of digital content | 5 years | Korean e-commerce law |
| Consumer complaints and disputes | 3 years | Korean e-commerce law |
| Access logs | Up to 3 months | Security and applicable legal requirements |
| Account and gameplay records | Until account closure | Service delivery |

If specific information must be retained after account closure to prevent abuse, the affected information, period and basis will be disclosed separately.

## 4. Third-party disclosure

Personal information is not disclosed to third parties without separate consent unless a specific law permits or requires it. Before a new disclosure, the Operator will explain the recipient, purpose, information and retention period and obtain consent where required.

For Kakao login, the Operator receives only the information the user agrees to share through Kakao. An app marketplace or payment provider selected during checkout may process payment information directly; the applicable provider and information are shown during checkout.

## 5. Processors and international transfers

If processing is outsourced or information is transferred outside Korea, the Operator will disclose the processor or recipient, purpose, information, country, timing and method and retention period as required, and apply appropriate safeguards. This section will be updated before any newly disclosable outsourcing or international transfer begins.

## 6. Deletion

- Information is classified for deletion when its purpose or retention period ends.
- Legally retained information is segregated and not used for another purpose.
- Electronic files are securely deleted so they cannot reasonably be restored; paper records are shredded or incinerated.

## 7. Your rights

Users may request access, transmission where applicable, correction, deletion, suspension of processing or withdrawal of consent. Requests can be made through account settings or the email below and will be handled after reasonable identity verification. A legal representative may act with appropriate proof of authority.

- Email: [ze2@kakao.com](mailto:ze2@kakao.com)

## 8. Cookies

Cookies or similar technologies may be used for login sessions, security and service improvement. Users can reject or delete cookies through browser settings, although some login features may then be unavailable. If behavioral data is used for personalized advertising, the Operator will separately disclose the data, method, purpose, retention period and opt-out method.

## 9. Security

Reasonable safeguards include least-privilege access, password encryption, transport protection, access-log management, security updates and backups.
